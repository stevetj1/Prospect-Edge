# Prospect Edge

A data-driven baseball card investment research and portfolio management platform.

## Mission
Identify undervalued baseball prospects, find profitable buying opportunities, monitor card values, and simplify the selling process.

## Initial Strategy
- Focus: 1st Bowman Chrome autographs
- Card types: Base, refractor, and numbered parallels
- Players: Primarily hitters
- Maximum purchase: $75 all-in
- Starting buying budget: $250
- Monthly realized net-profit goal: $500

## Core Features
- Prospect research and scoring
- eBay listing monitoring and buying alerts
- Individual card inventory and cost tracking
- Market valuations and selling alerts
- Historical data analysis and predictive modeling
- Automated listing preparation

## Development Approach
Build in small daily sessions, starting with a personal-use system before considering a commercial product.
